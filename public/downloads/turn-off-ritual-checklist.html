Turn-Off Ritual Checklist

Present Provider

Work Shutdown

  • Close all open browser tabs and windows
  • Clear your physical desk — put everything in its place
  • Review today's accomplishments (not what you didn't finish)
  • Write down any unfinished items so your brain can let go
  • Set tomorrow's top 3 priorities
  • Block tomorrow's calendar for deep work (90 min min.)
  • Turn off work notifications (Slack, email, Teams)

Family Transition

  • Physically leave your workspace (close the door or put laptop away)
  • Take 3 deep breaths (in 4 counts, hold 4, out 6)
  • Say out loud: "Work is closed. I am present."
  • Change into "home" clothes if helpful
  • Greet your family with eye contact and a real question
  • Put your phone in the phone parking spot
