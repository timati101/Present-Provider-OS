AI Career Prompt Library

Present Provider — 15 ChatGPT Prompts for Your Career

Resume & LinkedIn

1. Resume Impact BoostReview my resume bullets below and rewrite them to emphasize impact and measurable results. Make each one start with a strong action verb: [paste bullets]
2. LinkedIn Headline OptimizerI'm a [role] with [X] years in [industry]. Write 5 LinkedIn headline options that include keywords recruiters search for.
3. About Section WriterWrite a 3-paragraph LinkedIn About section based on my background. Para 1: who I am, Para 2: key achievements, Para 3: what I'm looking for.

Interview Prep

4. Generate Likely QuestionsBased on this job description, generate the 10 most likely interview questions and suggest strong answer frameworks for each: [paste JD]
5. Mock InterviewerYou are a hiring manager for [role] at [company]. Interview me for the position. Ask one question at a time and give feedback after each answer.
6. Tell Me About YourselfHelp me craft a 90-second 'Tell me about yourself' answer using the Present-Past-Future framework.

Skill Building

7. Skill Gap AnalysisI want to become a [target role]. Analyze my current skills below and identify the 5 biggest gaps, with resources to close each: [paste skills]
8. Learning RoadmapCreate a 12-week learning roadmap to go from beginner to job-ready in [skill]. Include weekly goals and free resources.
9. Career Pivot AssessmentI'm a [current role] considering a pivot to [target field]. What transferable skills do I have, and what's a realistic timeline?

Salary Negotiation

10. Market Rate ResearchWhat's the typical salary range for a [role] in [city] with [X] years of experience? Include total comp breakdown.
11. Negotiation ScriptI have an offer for $[X]. Market rate is $[Y]. Write me a polite, professional negotiation email asking for $[Z].
12. Counteroffer PrepRole-play: you are my employer countering my salary request. I'll respond, and you critique my approach. Start now.

Career Pivot

13. Career Path ExplorerBased on my background as [role], suggest 5 career paths I could pursue in 3-5 years. For each: salary potential, WLB, and skill requirements.
14. 12-Month Career PlanCreate a month-by-month 12-month plan to transition from [current role] to [target role]. Include monthly milestones and measurable goals.
15. Personal Brand AuditReview my LinkedIn and resume. What story does my professional brand tell, and how can I make it more compelling?
