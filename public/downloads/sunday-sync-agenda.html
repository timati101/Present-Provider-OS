Sunday Sync Agenda

Present Provider — 15-Minute Weekly Spouse Sync

1. Review Last Week (3 min)

  • What went well this past week?
  • What was challenging?
  • What got missed or dropped?

2. Upcoming Week (5 min)

  • Appointments, deadlines, travel, kid events
  • Who needs what this week?
  • Any scheduling conflicts to resolve?

3. Family Wins (3 min)

  • Celebrate one win from last week — big or small
  • What's one thing you're each looking forward to?

4. Needs Discussion (3 min)

  • Anything that needs a longer conversation?
  • Anything unsaid that needs to be surfaced?

5. Prayer / Focus (1 min)

  • Share one intention or prayer for the week ahead
  • Close together — a hug, a word of appreciation
